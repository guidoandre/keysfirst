# Keysfirst: rental-deposit rules by country (research, 29 Sep 2026)

Status: research only, not legal advice. Six agents read primary sources where the fetch tools allowed it (BOE, Légifrance, Irish Statute Book, gesetze-im-internet.de, Rijksoverheid, Normattiva) and marked secondary sources. Items in "Not verified" must be checked before any claim goes into the product. Re-check everything before a live launch.

## 1. What is true in all six countries

- **No country lets a landlord demand the deposit before the contract exists.** Germany: first instalment due at the start of the tenancy (§551(2) BGB). France: paid "au moment de la signature du bail" (art. 22 loi 89-462), and art. 22 allows payment "par l'intermédiaire d'un tiers". Spain: due on signing (LAU art. 36.1). Ireland: any payment "for the purpose of securing a tenancy" is capped (RTA 2004 s.19B). Italy: money paid before the contract is a *caparra* (art. 1385 c.c.), not a *deposito cauzionale*. Netherlands: no rule found.
  - Keysfirst's early lock is therefore a voluntary, refundable pre-payment. The copy must never say a landlord may require it.
- **The landlord's statutory duties start after release.** Keysfirst pays the landlord at the handover; the country's deposit law then governs what the landlord must do with the money (hold it apart, pay interest, register it, return it by a deadline). The product must say so and must not present the escrow as the legal deposit scheme.
- **The regulatory question is EU-wide.** Payment-institution and MiCA licensing does not differ by country; each report flagged it as unresolved. It stays with the BaFin check already planned.

## 3. Country-specific duties the interface can surface

**Germany**
- Anmeldung within 14 days (§17 BMG); landlord must give the Wohnungsgeberbestätigung (§19 BMG, fine up to €1,000).
- Subletting needs the main landlord's consent (§540 BGB). Fixed terms need a written statutory reason (§575 BGB). Leases over one year must be in writing (§550).
- Agent fees: Bestellerprinzip (§2 WoVermRG). Energy certificate at viewing (GEG §80).
- Not verified: whether a remote tenant who never viewed has a Fernabsatz withdrawal right (§312); whether third-party escrow counts as the "Sicherheit"; Zweckentfremdung rules per city.

**Netherlands**
- Wet goed verhuurderschap: written contract, written information on rights, municipal meldpunt. Middleman may not charge the tenant.
- Rooms always fall in the social sector: points system (WWS) sets the maximum rent (Wet betaalbare huur, from 1 Jul 2024).
- Temporary contracts: default is open-ended since 1 Jul 2024. Students (also from abroad) may get up to 2 years (art. 7:271(2) BW with Besluit specifieke groepen tijdelijke huurovereenkomst, Stb. 2024, 152). The jongerencontract (under 28, 5 years) is art. 7:274c and is a different thing.
- Signed start report matters: art. 7:224(2) BW presumes the tenant received the home in its end condition if no description was made.
- Subletting needs consent (art. 7:244). Room in the landlord's main home: part of tenant protection does not apply for the first 9 months (art. 7:232(3), secondary).
- Not verified: exact text of 7:261b, interest, BRP registration deadline.

**Ireland**
- A room in the landlord's own home is a licence, not a tenancy (RTA s.3(2)(g)): no RTB, no rent book, no minimum standards, landlord can end it with reasonable notice. This is the single most important warning for students.
- Public RTB register can be searched by Eircode, but only proves the address is registered, not that the advertiser owns it.
- Student-specific accommodation (PBSA) is covered by the Act (2019 Act s.37); the student advance-rent exception in s.19B(3) applies only where the same provider takes rent and tuition.
- National rent control since 1 Mar 2026 (Residential Tenancies (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2026: CPI or 2% cap; replaces Rent Pressure Zones). No effect on deposits.
- Gardaí: 230 accommodation scams in H1 2026, losses over €410k (RTE, 25 Aug 2026).
- Not verified: whether the cap covers licences outside student accommodation; whether an on-chain escrow counts as a payment "to secure a tenancy".

**Spain**
- Seasonal (temporada) study lets are *uso distinto* and carry the 2-month fianza. Whether a room is under the LAU or the Código Civil is contested; Madrid and INCASÒL treat rooms as covered for the fianza deposit.
- Agency and formalisation fees fall on the landlord for vivienda (art. 20.1 LAU, Ley 12/2023).
- Catalonia: Llei 11/2025 (from 1 Jan 2026) gives study/work seasonal lets a regime close to habitual housing and caps room rents (secondary).
- Registro Único de Arrendamientos: annulled by the Tribunal Supremo (STS 620/2026, May 2026); regional tourist registers still apply.
- Energy label attached to the lease (RD 390/2021 art. 17.2).
- Not verified: written-contract requirement, cédula de habitabilidad, empadronamiento and NIE details, other regions.

**France**
- Lease types: unfurnished (cap 1×), furnished / bail étudiant (9 months, cap 2×), bail mobilité (1 to 10 months, deposit forbidden).
- No fees before the lease is signed (art. 4 o); agent fees borne by the landlord except capped visit / file / drafting fees (art. 5).
- Visale (free, Action Logement) for students with a valid student visa; caution plus GLI is void except for student housing (art. 22-1).
- Home insurance proof due at key handover (art. 7 g).
- Rent control in Paris, Lille, Lyon and Villeurbanne, Montpellier, Bordeaux, Plaine Commune, Est Ensemble (experiment ends around 25 Nov 2026; extension not verified).
- APL for non-EU students changed on 1 Jul 2026 (Crous scholarship, work or apprenticeship required).
- Not verified: full text of arts. 22-1 and 25-7; 2026 fee-cap annex; cash rules.

**Italy**
- Contract types: student (L. 431/1998 art. 5(2), 6 to 36 months, tenant must be enrolled in a municipality other than their residence), transitorio (up to 18 months), 4+4. Written form mandatory (art. 1(4)).
- Registration with the Agenzia delle Entrate within 30 days, landlord's duty; unregistered leases are null (L. 311/2004 art. 1(346)) but late registration cures it.
- Tenant needs a codice fiscale (foreigners can obtain it via form AA4/8); landlord must register with it.
- APE energy class required in ads and contract. Cessione di fabbricato to the police within 48 hours (absorbed by registration, except non-EU nationals still need the communication under D.Lgs. 286/1998 art. 7).
- Renting to a foreigner without a valid permit is a crime (D.Lgs. 286/1998 art. 12(5-bis)): the landlord should see the visa or permit.
- Corrections to the brief: the cash ban on rent was repealed in 2016 (L. 208/2015); the general anti-money-laundering cash cap applies instead (€5,000, verify for 2026).
- Police advice: pay no more than 20% up front, only traceable, IBAN starting "IT", live video tour first.
- Not verified: caparra versus deposito for platform-held pre-contract money; the 2026 cash cap and cedolare rates; the comma structure of art. 13 L. 431/1998.

## 4. Interface decisions (29 Sep 2026)

Decided with the user:
- **Country is not stored.** The deal on Solana stays generic (title, amount, dates). Country only matters while the landlord creates the link, so nothing about it goes into the title or the program.
- **Amount above the legal cap is blocked** in the create flow (UI validation only, not an on-chain rule).
- **Scope before Sat 3 Oct:** country selector, cap check, "what the law says" step, FAQ section by country.
- **Later, if time allows:** deal-page checklists, landlord "Released" list. Both would need the country on the deal page (for example a `?c=` parameter on the share link, still not on-chain).
- **Wording:** "Europe" everywhere on the marketing pages. Countries appear in the FAQ ("Which countries does it cover?" and "Deposit rules by country"), not in the headlines.

Design:
- `web/src/content/countries.ts`: typed data for the six countries (housing options with cap in months, "what the law says" bullets).
- `web/src/lib/country-rules.ts`: cap arithmetic and messages, tested.
- `validateNewDeal` gains country, housing option and monthly rent, and returns an amount error above the cap.
- `CreateDealFlow`: step 1 asks country, (where it matters) rental type, rent and deposit with a live cap; step 3 shows "What the law says in {country}".
- FAQ: one entry per country generated from the same data.
