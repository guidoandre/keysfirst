Graphics: pictograms, not pictures

Built on a 48 px grid with 90° and 45° lines and round heads, in the Otl Aicher tradition. Ink plus one highlighter accent. Icons use a 2 px stroke with square caps.

Motion: a departure board, not a party

Short (150 to 250 ms), exact, ease-out. Status changes flip like a split-flap board; countdowns tick. Nothing loops, and reduced-motion settings skip every flip.

Tone of voice: calm, exact, fair

Plain, neutral register. Facts first, then the time, then what happens if it doesn't. No exclamation marks.

Honest trade-offs

Why it works

  • The hero is the rulebook: a visitor sees how the money moves before reading a word of marketing.
  • The calmest and most "financial" of the three; it reads as fair to landlords too.
  • Cheapest to build well by Thursday: type, rules and pictograms, with few custom illustrations.

What to watch

  • It can feel cool or bureaucratic if the copy gets lazy. The warmth lives in the highlighter and in plain, kind sentences.
  • Yellow with black can read as "caution". Yellow stays a highlighter, never a warning colour, and never large fields.
