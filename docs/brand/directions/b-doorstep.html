Doorstep

Moving abroad is a leap of faith. Keysfirst is the friendly hand at the door. The world: the doorstep of a new home, with its painted front door, key tag, doormat and suitcase, and that first evening in a room of your own.

Illustration: warm objects, no outlines

Flat, rounded shapes from the arrival: doors, keys with paper tags, suitcases, a plant by the step. No faces, so everyone fits in. Icons use a 2 px rounded stroke.

Motion: a key turns, a door opens

Gentle and a little springy (300 to 450 ms). Locking turns the key; releasing swings the door open onto warm light. Reduced-motion settings swap states with a fade.

  • Enter: 350 ms, soft spring, 12 px rise. Exit: 200 ms fade.
  • Released: the door swings open once; the screen warms to green.

Tone of voice: warm, reassuring, plain

Like a friend who has moved abroad before. Second person, short sentences, and the reassurance comes first. Friendly, never jokey about money.

Honest trade-offs

Why it works

  • The most human of the three. It speaks to a student's nerves before a big move.
  • Stands apart from banks and from crypto. The door and key tag make memorable pictures that also carry the pitch deck and video.

What to watch

  • Softer on seriousness. For €600 to feel safe, the copy has to be precise about rules and times.
  • Needs the most custom illustration (door, tag and handover scenes), so it's the most build time before Thursday.
  • Apricot only works as a big calm field behind navy text; overused, it gets sugary.
