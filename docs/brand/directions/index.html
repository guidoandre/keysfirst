Keysfirst: three brand directions

All three keep the promise "The deposit moves only when the keys do", plain English, the devnet label and the exact status labels. They differ in how they earn trust: through clarity (A), warmth (B) or confidence (C). Each board shows the logo idea, palette with contrast ratios, fonts, graphic style, motion, tone of voice, the landing hero (desktop and phone) and the deal page (tenant and landlord). Open them on your phone too.

Why A

  • Five-second test: its hero is the rulebook itself (pay, scan at the door, or money back after the deadline), so a visitor sees how the money moves before reading any marketing.
  • Trust for €600: it is the calmest and most "financial" of the three, and it reads as fair to landlords as well as tenants.
  • Thursday: type, rules and pictograms need far less custom illustration than B, and it avoids C's "yet another green money app" risk.
  • What it needs: warm, plain copy, and yellow used strictly as a highlighter, never as a warning.
